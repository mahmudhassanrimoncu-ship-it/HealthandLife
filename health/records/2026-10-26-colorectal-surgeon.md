## What it means for the diagnosis

- The bleeding on 23 and 24 Oct (and after the first Prodep day in Sep) now has a clear local cause: a chronic fissure. Both SSRIs still make bleeding easier, so the psychiatrist should still hear about it.
- A chronic fissure with a sentinel pile is a classic result of years of long straining. That supports the bathroom-loop explanation.
- Chronic fissures usually come with a tight, spasming internal anal sphincter. That fits the "pelvic floor that has learned to clench" explanation, and the anal spasms on the first Prodep day.

**Healing basics:** short toilet visits without straining, feet raised on a low stool, warm sitz baths, ispaghula with plenty of water.
